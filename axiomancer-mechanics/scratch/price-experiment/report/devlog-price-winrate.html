Does raising card price buy more wins?

In Axiomancer a PAID card line always costs exactly one die, so "pricing a card higher" means packing more status & burst power into that single die. We fanned out ~30,000 seeded encounters across all ten preset decks, four campaign stages, and a within-deck price ladder to find out where that extra power actually converts.

Verdict Partial — price is a three-regime lever, not a dial
Early · the ceiling
+6pts over ~7× price
Mid · it converts
+27pts, 41→68%
Late · the wall
4→12% even at 4×

What to do about it

Ranked. The lever works — once you price the thing that actually wins the fight.
    Method. Deterministic engine sims, blind policy (the doctrine curve), 40–60 runs × 2 seeds per cell. Win-rate = victory + mercy + capitulate + concede. Adversarially verified: the mid trend and the late wall survive; fine-grained monotonicity at seeds=2 does not — re-run with more seeds before trusting it.
