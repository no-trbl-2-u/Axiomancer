The Card Ledger

Every number on this page is the current game: Upgradeable Dice ON (the build every player gets), 10 starter presets × 8 policies × 4 stages × full enemy rosters, two seeds pooled — 172,800 simulated encounters, measured 2026-07-18 on tree 0f7f0500.

No comparisons to the pre-dice game. The library is judged against itself and the doctrine it must satisfy.

The doctrine curve, deck by deck

The band is the law: blind-pilot win rates of ~80% early, ~50% mid, 25–35% late, 0% impossible. A working library keeps all ten lines inside the grey steps. Today of ten decks clear the early band, one (Oratory) clears mid, none clear late — and Oratory beats the unwinnable fight, which the doctrine forbids.

Status play — the main fun, measured

Share of card plays that land a status effect on the enemy, per deck (all stages, runs-weighted). The doctrine says status is the point of combat. Foundry and Grace never touch it — they win entirely through SWAY capitulation, a different game wearing the same rules.

Card triage — where drawing the card changes the game

For the 18 cards with a clean signal: horizontal axis is dWR — how much your win rate moves in runs where the card reaches your hand vs runs (same deck, same fight) where it doesn't. Vertical axis is fizz — attempts eaten by the dice gate. Dot size is play volume. Left of the line, the card is dead weight you sometimes draw; high up, it's a promise the dice keep breaking.

Staples locked in one deck

The duplication precedent already exists: venom-and-vein rides in three presets and is the library's only strongly positive-dWR multi-deck card (+4.9%). These thirteen staples land statuses reliably, carry low rules load (cx ≤ 6), and are each fielded by a single preset — the cheapest inventory for repairing the failing decks without authoring new cards.

Reading the instruments

  1. dWR is estimated per cell (same stage, enemy, policy, deck) and pooled, so it never compares a card against a different fight. Only 18 of 60 fielded cards produce the contrast (the rest are drawn in virtually every run). A residual bias remains: fast wins end before the deck is seen, which deflates dWR slightly for everyone — read strong negatives as real, mild ones as suspicion.
  2. Status-land rate ≈ paid-line share on most engines because the status rides the PAID line; the FREE line is the drip. That's the design, not a coincidence in the data.
  3. statusEngagement is enemy-side only. SWAY/RAPPORT play (Grace, Foundry, Bastion's sway half) lands on the player's own board and doesn't count. Grace's 0% is partly the instrument; Foundry's 0% is the deck.
  4. Opportunity rate is saturated (every card ≥ 90% played-when-drawn) because sim pilots hold nothing back — it will only become a cut signal once a card-holding policy exists.
  5. Seed noise floor: mean |Δwin| 0.9%, max 6.1% between seeds. Every claim on this page clears it several times over.
  6. Source data: docs/reports/preset-metrics/2026-07-18-flagon-seed{1,101}.json; reading: plan/tuning/2026-07-18-card-library-metrics-accumulation.md. Deterministic — the stamped commands regenerate byte-identical.
