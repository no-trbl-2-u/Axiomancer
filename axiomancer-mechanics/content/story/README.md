# THERE IS NO STORY

**Status as of 2026-09-18: this game has no story canon, and no law
about what its story is.** That is not an oversight or a gap waiting to
be filled by whoever reads this next. It is a deliberate, T-directed
clearing of the ground, and it holds until T says otherwise.

T, in the attended session that ordered it:

> Remove ALLL law about what the story is. We're starting from square
> one with an unidentifieable "x" as the first/main character.

## What that means, concretely

- **There is no arc.** No premise, no inciting incident, no ending, no
  theme the game is "about."
- **There is no protagonist.** The player is **X** — no name, no figure,
  no identifiers. Not a boy, not an adult, not a child, no gender, no
  body, no station, no trade, no family. `X` is a placeholder used while
  the story is being brainstormed. It is not a name, and it is not a
  working title for a name chosen later.
- **There are no canonical characters.** No one in this game has a fixed
  identity, history, or relationship to anyone else.
- **There is no world-story.** No kings, no rites, no institutions, no
  hundred-year cycles — nothing at that layer is settled.

## The shipped content is NOT canon

The game currently ships ~186 dialogue nodes across seven playable maps,
plus NPCs, map-event prose, and 21 story flags named `boy-*`. **None of
it is story law.** It is legacy text that happens to be in the build.
It stays playable because deleting it would break a working game for no
gain, not because it is true.

Specifically:

- A shipped line is **evidence of what a previous draft assumed**, never
  evidence of what the story is.
- The `boy-*` flag names are **identifiers, not a claim that the player
  is a boy.** Renaming them is deferred engine work, not a story
  decision.
- A future outline may keep, move, rewrite, or discard any of it freely.
  No shipped beat has standing.

## Rules for any agent or session reading this

1. **Do not invent canon.** If a task needs a story fact that does not
   exist, the answer is that it does not exist. Say so and stop.
2. **Do not treat shipped content as canon**, and do not reconstruct an
   arc from it. Reading the maps to know what is there is fine.
   Concluding what the story "is" from them is not.
3. **Do not restore a removed document** or write a replacement for one
   on your own initiative.
4. **Do not give X a name, figure, age, gender, or family.** Not in
   prose, not in a spec, not in an example, not in passing.
5. **Voice and craft rules still apply** — `docs/narrative/` governs
   *how* shipped copy is written. Those are style law, not story law,
   and they survive this clearing untouched.
6. **Mechanics are untouched.** LOCKED MECHANICS (Conviction, Surge,
   Dice) and every combat/world system are unaffected. This clearing is
   about narrative only.

## What happens next

A new story overview will be built from nothing, event by event, in
attended sessions with T. The method is written up in
`plan/2026-09-17-story-outline.prompt.md`: one event per turn, four
pitches plus T's write-in, recorded and committed each turn, until the
road is long enough to reason about from above.

When that file exists, **it** becomes the overview — and this document
is superseded by it, not merged with it.

Until then: there is no story.
