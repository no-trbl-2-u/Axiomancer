Across 5 A/B cycles in this continuous-tuning PR, **all 23 legal registry levers
were A/B-tested and rejected.** The two difficulty knobs (`enemy.statPerLevel`,
`enemy.gearTierPerLevel`) were the ONLY levers that improved aggregate health,
and re-testing `enemy.statPerLevel → 2.5` at `--runs 150` confirmed the verdict:
improvement **0.0120** vs CI margin **0.0148** — directionally positive, no
witness/engagement regression, but **not significant**.

**Mechanism (the conclusive part):** the significance test is a paired test over
the **36 matrix cells** (`n=36`), not over the run count. Raising `--runs` from 50
to 150 shrank per-cell noise (margin 0.0170 → 0.0148) but the improvement estimate
shrank in lockstep (0.0137 → 0.0120). The residual margin is dominated by
**between-cell variance**: the benefit of every helpful lever is concentrated in
the ~12 L1 cells and is ~zero in the 24 saturated L15/L30 cells, so a global lever's
mean effect is diluted below significance across the full matrix. **No amount of
runs fixes this — it is structural.** Any global numeric lever whose benefit is
localized to a difficulty/level subset will be rejected by the full-matrix paired
test by construction.

**Actionable (non-numeric) follow-ups for T:**
1. **Make the optimizer (or the change) level-conditioned.** Either tune per
   difficulty-tier (so an L1-concentrated benefit isn't averaged against saturated
   high-level cells) or add a level-conditioned lever (L1-only HP buffer / a
   level-scaled status-resolution bonus). `enemy.statPerLevel → 2.5` is a genuine
   directional win for L1 that the global-matrix test cannot bank — worth a
   human-judged manual apply if L15/L30 easing is acceptable.
2. **Registry bug:** `effect.resolutionDebuffIntensityThreshold` has `min 4` but
   `current 3` → its "lowers difficulty" direction is unreachable. Lower the min.
3. **L1-hard band/roster:** the cliff is uniform across all 4 playstyles → an
   encounter-tier scaling step or a mis-set L1-hard band floor (0.50), not
