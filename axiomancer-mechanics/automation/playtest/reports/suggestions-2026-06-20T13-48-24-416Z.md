## Propose-only (needs human judgement)

_From balance-analyst subagent + this tick's A/B evidence:_

**Empirical finding this tick — symmetric effect tuning cannot fix L1-hard.**
All six analyst candidates (`tier1_heart_defend.healthPerRound` 2→3,
`debuff_confusion.duration` 4→6, `debuff_fear.duration` 3→4,
`debuff_bleed.damagePerRound` 3→4, `maxDuration` 10→13,
`resolutionDebuffIntensityThreshold` 3→4) were A/B-tested under the frozen
ruler and **all reverted** — most as "baseline healthier" (i.e. the change
helped the enemy at least as much as the player), one as not-significant.
Because every tunable in the registry is symmetric (effects apply to both
combatants), buffing DoT/control/heal also arms the L1-hard enemy roster, so
these knobs cannot net-relieve the L1-hard collapse. This moves the
"asymmetric scaling" idea below from speculation to an evidence-backed wall.

- **Asymmetric player-vs-enemy effect scaling (root cause).** The symmetric
  registry is the structural reason L1-hard collapses (L1-strategist-hard
  0.08 / 92% defeat vs `the-market-arbiter`; L1-mixed-hard 0.23 / 84% defeat
  vs `reef-barnacle-colony`) while L1 easy/normal over-resolve (0.82–1.0). A
  player-side multiplier on tier-1 effect potency (or an enemy-side damper)
  would relieve L1-hard without over-easing the already-too-easy easy/normal
  cells. Not reachable by any registry id — needs a new tunable or a structural
  resolver change.
- **L1 enemy stat / HP curve compression.** L1 difficulty is *compressed*, not
  merely hard: easy 0.82–1.0, hard 0.08–0.49. This implies the L1 hard enemy
  roster (`the-market-arbiter`, `reef-barnacle-colony`, `thicket-ambusher`) is
  budgeted far above the easy/normal roster rather than on a smooth slope.
  `enemy.statPerLevel` is global and cannot localize a fix to L1.
- **Witness-gap root cause — engaged but not converting (new).** Strategist
  leverage is now ~63% (above the 0.35 floor) yet strategist *resolution* still
  trails aggressive by 11% (81% vs 92%). Status play is being *applied* but not
  *converting* to wins — a shift from the 2026-06-08 tick, where the problem was
  engagement *below* floor (20/36 cells). The resolution routes (erosion /
  saturation thresholds vs authored effect potency) appear mistuned relative to
  enemy HP at L1-hard. Worth a structural pass on the effect-resolution route
  gating, not numeric nudges.
- **L30-defensive-hard timeout (`cindergeist-revenant`).** 10% resolution / 90%
  timeout / 76.7 avg rounds with healthy 33 skill uses/run — classic
  inert-spam-into-timeout. Likely a rounds-to-kill horizon mismatch on a
  high-HP enemy; the relevant knobs (`resolutionDotMaxRoundsToKill`,
  poison damage) are all on cooldown this tick.
