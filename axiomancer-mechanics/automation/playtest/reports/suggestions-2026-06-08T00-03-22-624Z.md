  not add a timeout-grading rule).** The player should not be stuck sitting in
  combat that long. Fix the timeout problem from the *other* side: make skills
  and status effects strong/decisive enough that status play resolves fights
  **before** the existing round cap. This is the doctrine-aligned direction and
  sets the focus for the next tuning tick (effect potency / DoT damage / skill
  strength / the resolution thresholds). The kept change this tick
  (`resolutionDebuffIntensityThreshold` 8 → 6) is the first step on that path.
- **Enemy effective-HP / damage-sponge audit → see above.** Do not nerf the
  enemies structurally or raise the cap; make status effects potent enough to
  chew through the L30+ sponges within the cap.
- **Late-hard defeat spikes → DEFERRED.** Address later with a focused
  combat-tuning run targeting `the-last-consensus`, `warrant-of-the-void`,
  and `reef-barnacle-colony` individually.

_From the `balance-analyst` subagent. The band term (0.1118) is ~85% of total
health; the dominant failure mode is **timeouts, not defeats** — combat reaches
the round cap before resolving even when the player is winning the damage race by
orders of magnitude. Engagement is already healthy (all four playstyles clear the
35% floor). The lever is making status play actually **close out** fights._

- **Round cap / timeout-resolution policy (highest leverage, NOT in registry).**
  The ~80-round cap produces timeouts as the dominant non-resolution across 20+
  cells where the player is clearly ahead (`l15-mixed-easy` 1007:1 damage yet 100%
  timeout; `l30-mixed-normal` 23.65:1 yet 100% timeout). Either raise the cap, or —
  better — add a timeout-resolution rule that grades a timed-out fight by
  accumulated damage/control state rather than scoring it as a flat
  non-resolution. Reclassifies many "winning but slow" cells into band without
  touching combat power. Evidence: every cell with `timeoutRate ≥ 0.6` and
  `playerToEnemy ≫ 1`.

- **Enemy effective-HP / damage-sponge audit at L30+ (content-level, NOT a single
  tunable).** Several enemies are unkillable sponges regardless of player skill:
  `apostate-abbot` (0% res, both L30 cells), `the-tithewarden` (2% res),
  `graveward-keeper` / `the-schismarch` / `iron-covenanter` / `cathedral-of-doubt`
  (all ~0% res, ~100% timeout). These read as per-enemy HP/defense outliers, not a
  global-scaling miss — `enemy.statPerLevel` is too blunt and would over-nerf the
  cells that are fine. Inspect these specific enemy definitions.

- **Punishing late hard matchups — genuine defeat spikes (NOT auto-fixable
  safely).** `l50-aggressive-hard` (98% defeat, `the-last-consensus`),
  `l50-strategist-hard` (82% defeat, `warrant-of-the-void`), `l1-defensive-hard`
  (68% defeat, `reef-barnacle-colony`) are real losses, not timeouts. Localized
  encounter blowouts; any global difficulty knob that fixes them trivializes the
  many normal cells already in/above band. Review these three encounters
  individually.

- **Easy cells stuck at 0–40% resolution (`l15-mixed-easy`, `l50-mixed-easy`).**
  Easy difficulty timing out (`l15-mixed-easy` 100% timeout vs `apprentice-heretic`
  at 1007:1) is the timeout-policy bug at its most glaring — same root cause as
  item 1, flagged separately because an easy cell stuck at 0% resolution is the
  most visible band failure in the matrix.

_Note: the kept change (`resolutionDebuffIntensityThreshold` 8 → 6) is the
numeric down payment on item 1 — it lets debuff-saturated strategist play force a
yield before the cap. The structural timeout-resolution rule is the full fix._
