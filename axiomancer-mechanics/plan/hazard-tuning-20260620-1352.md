## 3. Change decision — propose-only (nothing applied)

- **Starter bag:** in-band on both harnesses → **no change.**
- **Acquired-utility (vow/CHOOSE) trap:** the 2026-06-16 tick measured that
  acquiring gold-vow (`r_vow`, `x_goldoath`, `x_brightpromise`) and CHOOSE
  (`r_twin`, `x_pendulum`, `x_eithergate`) cards makes a deck **worse** (−1.0 safe /
  −0.65 risk avg wins, across all 6 hazards), the opposite of the doctrine that
  utility play should beat flat trading. Re-checked this tick: those cards **still
  carry 0/0 free rows** in `hazard.content.ts`, so the finding is unresolved. The
  proposed fix is structural (give them a small non-zero free row, and/or relax the
  gold-gate) — **propose-only, still awaiting T.** Full analysis and the exact
  card-by-card recommendation live in `plan/hazard-tuning-20260616-1606.md` §4.
  Nothing is re-applied here; flagging that it is still open.

No numeric lever in the tunable registry fixes the trap without adding variance
to an already feast-or-famine card (see prior report §4 "Considered but not
applied"), so applying a magnitude bump would be tuning around a content-shape
gap — disallowed. Hence report-only this tick.

### Considered but not applied

- **Starter deck reweight toward utility** (lift the lone convert card / lower a
  red+blue common to push the ~64%-by-weight pure-number share below 50%). The
  intent — fewer flat rounds — is doctrine-aligned, but its *only measurable*
  effect on the shipped harness is the greedy clear rate (which would likely drop,
  risking the safe band), while the metric it is meant to improve (**flat-round
  rate / convert-fire rate**) is **not emitted by any shipped harness**. Applying it
  blind would be unvalidated against its own objective. Left for after the harness
  candidate (below) lands instrumentation for those metrics.
- **`dice.faces` gold/hex retune.** High blast radius, lifts the band-guarded
  starter balance; too risky to apply blind in a sweep tick. Same conclusion as
  2026-06-16.
