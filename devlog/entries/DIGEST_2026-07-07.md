## [mechanics] Phase 14 — route survivorship/coverage-audit classification

**What:** `game.cli --route` now classifies every run as survivorship,
blocked, or coverage-audit; it halts the instant a combat encounter ends
in defeat instead of walking past it, and a new `--route-audit` flag
gives a non-mutating full-map coverage witness.
**Why:** promoted 2026-07-04 from a field playthrough report — the old
route reached only 19/25 Fishing Village nodes, couldn't prove full
branch coverage in one legal run, and kept reporting post-defeat
traversal as clean survivorship.

## [mechanics] Phase 3 — enemy rage-mode threat phase

**What:** enemies without an authored threat sequence now get a locked
4th "rage" phase (harder-hitting + self-healing) that unlocks after
round 6; `processBetweenPhases` holds the phase pointer at the last
reachable phase while it's locked.
**Why:** doctrine-central — long fights should get qualitatively harder
over time, rewarding fast status finishes. Scoped to generated sequences
only; the 51 hand-authored, playtest-calibrated sequences are untouched.
**Commits:** e87559d0, f2d59a13
