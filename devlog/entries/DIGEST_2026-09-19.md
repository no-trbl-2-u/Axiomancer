## [mechanics] A combat bug: automated witnesses were folding to mercy offers they should have fought through

**What:** Both the playtest matrix and the CLI's `--auto` mode
unconditionally accepted any capitulation (RELENT) offer — no automated
run could ever fight through one, even with the enemy at single-digit HP.
This silently converted near-certain kills into mercy endings, inflating
the matrix's mercy share for every policy except `mercy-seeker`/`chaos`,
and let The Incompleteness's impossible-stage ceiling probe leak through a
mercy exit 5 times out of 6 instead of holding. Fixed by giving every sim
policy its own `capitulationChoice: 'accept' | 'continue'` stance — only
`mercy-seeker` (mercy is its identity) and `chaos` (the noise floor)
accept; every other witness now plays through the offer toward its own
win condition, per the "alt-wins compete on merit, none protected"
doctrine. Landed alongside **FLURRY** (Phase 90), a new multi-hit enemy
keyword ("clause by clause"), first carrier Guild Knife.

**Why:** the sim's job is to measure the game honestly; a policy that
can't decline mercy can't tell you whether a fight is actually beatable
head-on.

**Commits:** `87be9d91` (PR #330), `39e2915e` (Phase 90, FLURRY)
