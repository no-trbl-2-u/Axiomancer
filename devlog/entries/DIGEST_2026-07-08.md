**What:** enchant/disenchant cards were PAID-only persistent passives with no
FREE line — playing one felt inert (their effects are subtle, level-10+ engine
hooks). They now carry BOTH lines: the FREE (dieless) line grants a **timed
3-round** instance of the same passive; the PAID line makes the identical
passive **permanent** (rest of combat, unique-in-play, leaves the deck cycle). A

— only the duration differs.

the whole fight" split makes these cards do something visible immediately, and
gives the die a real decision instead of a mandatory tax.
