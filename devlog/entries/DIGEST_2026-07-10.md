## [mobile] Labyrinth crash on battle defeat fixed

**What:** losing a battle inside THE APORIA no longer trips the global error
boundary. `resetRun` now clears the transient labyrinth session slice (the
single choke point for every run reset), the presenter falls back to

the map when its session is cleared out from under it.
