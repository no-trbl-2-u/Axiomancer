## [mechanics] Phase 32 parts 4e-4f — Charm resolve milestones and Echo's ouroboros retarget

**What:** Charm gets a two-stage SWAY arc — `swayResolveMilestoneThresholds`
computes Wavering (45%) and Faltering (80%) as *fractions of the enemy's
live capitulate threshold*, not fixed numbers, so the waypoints track a
shrinking or growing resolve pool correctly. Each fires at most once per
combat via latching booleans (a live denominator can't be re-derived from
a before/after comparison the way other themes' ledgers can); Wavering
pays a RAPPORT stack, Faltering a small unscaled SWAY bonus. Echo's
Ouroboros (`replay_last`) now only updates its retarget on a PAID spell
that actually landed or deepened a status — a fizzle, a ledger-only
burst, or a dieless no-op no longer steals the echo away from the last
spell that did something real.
**Why:** closes out Phase 32's per-theme CONFIRMED items across all ten
themes — Echo had no CONFIRMED-M item, so its best CONFIRMED-S item (the
retarget fix) stood in, same cut every other part took. Only Part 1b
(cross-run Souls persistence, deliberately never bundled into any prior
part) is left before the phase's `[ ]` flips to `[x]`.
**Commits:** 974e15e3, 09d92fe7
