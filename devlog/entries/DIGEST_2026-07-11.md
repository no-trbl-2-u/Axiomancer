## [mechanics] The Turn Law — one dice-turn per threat phase, and the honest baseline it revealed (phases 26-27)

**What:** `startTurn` now refuses a second dice tray within the same threat
phase (a `turnTakenThisPhase` flag, cleared when `processBetweenPhases` opens
the next phase); sim policies, the auto CLI, and the live mobile-map
auto-runner all stopped farming `endTurn`/`startTurn` cycles for extra
Conviction and instead spend down the rest of the hand via FREE (dieless) top
plays. A companion fix clamps damage attribution to the enemy's actual
remaining HP so a long DoT chain can't log more damage than the enemy could
ever take. Phase 27 then re-ran the full stage × policy matrix (144 cells,
8,640 fights) under the new law: mid-stage win rate was previously read at
94% (farm-inflated), honestly it's 3%; late was read at 3%, honestly 0% flat
across all 6 enemies × 8 policies. Early holds close to doctrine (75% vs
~80% target) with no farming needed.
**Why:** the 2026-07-10 combat-engagement audit (33-agent fan-out: 10 theme
playthrough auditors, 5 cross-cutting lenses, adversarial skeptics, a
completeness critic) found the engine had no round-turn law at all — every
pre-2026-07-10 balance number in `plan/tuning/` was measured against sims
that farmed ~4.7 dice-turns per enemy phase. Phase 26 closes the exploit;
Phase 27 proves what the game actually plays like without it, so the
repricing phases (28-33) tune against real numbers instead of farmed ones.
**Commits:** f849c5a2 (phase 26), 30e43eb2 (phase 27)

## [ui] Equipment dock layout — four passes to get portrait + 5 slots right

**What:** the equipment dock collapsed 3 interchangeable accessory
positions into one "TRINKET" row, so a 2nd/3rd equipped accessory was
invisible (read as equip/unequip being broken). Fixed to render all 5 worn
slots (Weapon, Armor, Trinket I/II/III) with the player's chosen portrait in
place of the generic PaperDoll SVG (now deleted) — then three follow-up
passes reflowed the layout: portrait moved left and enlarged, slot width

**Why:** the engine already models the full 5-piece loadout; the dock's
presentation was silently hiding 2 of 5 slots. The layout took four
iterations to land because each fix exposed the next constraint (portrait
size fighting slot width, names truncating) rather than being scoped upfront.
