## [mechanics] Phase 26 — the Turn Law

**What:** `startTurn` now refuses a second dice-tray roll within the same
threat phase (a new `turnTakenThisPhase` invariant); `resolveThreatPhase`
re-arms it at the phase boundary. Card plays, floats, and Reserve spends
stay ungated — only the tray-roll is capped once per phase.
**Why:** sim policies, autoplay, and the CLI had been silently exploiting
an `endTurn`→`startTurn` loop to farm extra dice-trays per phase, which is
what every pre-Turn-Law win-rate curve was actually measuring.
`KNOWN_CURVE_VIOLATORS` dropped from 3 entries to 0 once the exploit
closed — every starter preset now passes curve shape honestly.
**Commits:** 6ea123fc
