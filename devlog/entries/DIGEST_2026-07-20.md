## [mechanics] THE COVETED DIE — phase 33c

**What:** boss and unique threat phases can now carry a stake: a die
the boss steals on the 2nd step of all 18 authored boss/unique
sequences (14 bosses + 4 uniques) unless the player answers the
stance-check, blocks it, or staggers the phase to 0 first. The steal
mirrors the momentum-surge temp-gold-die payout exactly (table-full
overflow converts to +1 Conviction) and is capped once per phase
index per combat so a repeating final phase can't be farmed.
**Why:** closes #110 — gives named threats a mechanical "stakes"
identity beyond damage numbers, reusing the existing payout/overflow
plumbing rather than inventing a new one. Inert while the
upgradeable-dice flag is off; no mobile UI yet (rides with a later
follow-up).
**Commits:** fc7fddbf, 345cb0a6
