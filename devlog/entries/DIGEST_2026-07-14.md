## [combat] SWAY capitulation becomes a real choice; damage receipts get truthful

**What:** reaching an enemy's SWAY resolve threshold no longer ends
the fight automatically — it opens an explicit ACCEPT / CONTINUE
choice (wired into both the CLI's interactive and auto loops), matching
how the mercy/exploit choice already works. `buildCombatSummary`'s
attribution now folds in any enemy healing the log recorded before
computing direct-damage share, so the post-fight receipt can't silently
overstate DoT's cut. Keyword atlas, spec-32 card-library text, and the
card editor's SWAY blurb were all reworded to describe the choice
instead of the old auto-CAPITULATE language.
**Why:** the old auto-capitulate behavior was already stale — the
mercy/exploit choice pattern this now matches shipped first, and the
copy across three surfaces (atlas, spec, editor) still described the
retired behavior.
**Commits:** ee837622, ddec4adc

## [combat] Keep-hand rule — hand size 5, round boundary refills instead of redrawing

**What:** `COMBAT_HAND_SIZE` dropped 6→5, and the round boundary no
longer discards the hand and draws fresh — unplayed cards are kept and
the hand refills up to target, so holding a dead card now costs real
draw room until it's played or scrapped. Bonus-draw riders raise the
refill target instead of riding a full redraw; the WS2.1 one-use law is
unchanged (an unplayed CONJURED Thoughtform still evaporates at the
boundary). A new regression test witnesses the boundary directly: held
uids survive, and the refill draws exactly the gap back up to 5.
**Why:** the old fresh-hand redraw was quietly wiping seed-payoff arcs
players spent cards building — diagnosed in the 2026-07-13
spell-grammar brainstorm (braindumped alongside this change), which
also flagged FREE-line seed monotony and thin enchant/curse density as
follow-on design directions.
**Commits:** da26ac5f, 842efde8
