[combat] Keep-hand rule — hand size 5, round boundary refills instead of redrawing

What: COMBAT_HAND_SIZE dropped 6→5, and the round boundary no longer discards the hand and draws fresh — unplayed cards are kept and the hand refills up to target, so holding a dead card now costs real draw room until it's played or scrapped. Bonus-draw riders raise the refill target instead of riding a full redraw; the WS2.1 one-use law is unchanged (an unplayed CONJURED Thoughtform still evaporates at the boundary). A new regression test witnesses the boundary directly: held uids survive, and the refill draws exactly the gap back up to 5. Why: the old fresh-hand redraw was quietly wiping seed-payoff arcs players spent cards building — diagnosed in the 2026-07-13 spell-grammar brainstorm (braindumped alongside this change), which also flagged FREE-line seed monotony and thin enchant/curse density as follow-on design directions.
