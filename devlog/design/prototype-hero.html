✠ Hero reveal — what the holes do

Three scroll behaviours for the holed sheet over a real Doré plate (assets/images/maps/forest-dark.webp). The sheet is the generated hero-sheet.svg; drop a hand-cut one in its place and every treatment still works. Reduced motion, in all three: no transform at all — the sheet cross-fades to the plate over the same scroll distance, which is why the title never rides on the animation.

1a — parallax drift (ships)

The plate behind travels slower than the sheet in front, so the world moves inside the holes while the sheet holds still. Nothing changes shape — the depth does the work.

1b — holes bloom open

The sheet scales past the frame, so every hole widens until the plate has eaten the page. The most dramatic, and the most reliant on the sheet's edge quality: at small sizes it reads as a filter effect rather than a world.

1c — sheet lifts away

The sheet is torn upward off a plate that barely moves — a page turning. Cheapest to compute and calmest on a phone, but it says “a page turned” rather than “there is a world behind this”.
