# Phase 36a — Alt-win-aware card pricing (`scoreCard` v2, part 1)

> Generated by `/march` → `/ship-a-phase` (brief was missing). Design
> grounded in `scratch/price-experiment/report/FINDINGS.md` rec #3, the
> live pricing model (`cards.pricing.ts` + `pricing.engine.test.ts`), the
> CAPITULATE/CONCEDE thresholds (`Combat/effects.ts`), and a
> `mechanics-expert` design pass (2026-07-17). Enemy-independent scope
> only — the enemy-gated tempo half is Phase 36b.

## Outcome

`scoreCard` — the spell power-budget lint — stops under-pricing the two
**enemy-independent** alt-win engines it can actually see per-card:

1. **SWAY currency** (the CAPITULATE track) reprices `0.8 → 0.9` per stack.
2. **CONCEDE** (the Peroration alt-win) gains a **flat +3 capstone** on the
   declaring card — today the concede win prices at exactly **0** (the
   `peroration` case scores only its `at`-payoff rider, never the
   `concedeAt` win).

This is a **rank-honesty** fix (the lint's existing job), not a repurposing
of the lint into a win-rate predictor. A rank-5 charm finisher that ends the
game (`the-closing-word`, currently 8.80 — near the Axiom floor while
literally winning) should not price like a control cantrip.

**Explicitly deferred (see Follow-ups):** enchant/disenchant persistent
passives still score 0. They carry their effect as **free prose**
(`persistentEffect: string`), not a scoreable payload — pricing them needs a
schema change to structure that field into machine-readable mechanics, which
is its own phase (filed as **36c**). So 36a closes the SWAY-currency and
concede holes, NOT the enchant-engine hole; the alt-win gap is *partially*
closed by design.

## The load-bearing insight — SWAY and Premise are NOT symmetric

Thresholds (verified in `src/Combat/effects.ts`):

- **CAPITULATE:** `capitulateThreshold = min(max(10, round(0.35 ×
  maxHealth)), currentHP)`; SWAY decays 1/turn; the win is **emergent** at a
  turn boundary — **no declaring card**.
- **CONCEDE:** flat Premise floor `CONCEDE_PREMISES_BASE = 8` (elite 10, boss
  12), fired by a **single declaring card** (`the-closing-word`).

Win-conversion arithmetic against an enemy of `H` max HP (pricing law:
1 pt ≈ 3 HP, so a kill = `H/3` pts):

- **SWAY:** the win costs `0.35·H` stacks → parity per stack =
  `(H/3)/(0.35·H) = 0.95`. **`H` cancels — the per-stack win-value is
  enemy-independent**, so repricing the *currency* is principled. Take a
  ~5–15 % haircut for decay waste (worst early, where the floor-10 /
  current-HP cap bites) → **0.9**. (Dawncaster's `Charmed` is the same
  shape — resolve as a fraction of target HP; our 35 % model descends from
  it.)
- **Premise:** the win costs a flat `8` tally → per-tally win-value =
  `(H/3)/8 = H/24` — **scales with enemy HP**. There is no enemy-independent
  per-Premise win price, and most Premises cash to the smaller peroration
  riders (not to a concede), so repricing the *currency* would double-count
  AND be enemy-dependent. Instead the concede win-value belongs as a **lump
  on the declaring card**, not smeared across the currency. Premise stays a
  0.8 *build* currency (like `soul` at 0.75).

## Decisions made upfront — DO NOT ASK

1. **SWAY → 0.9, not 0.95.** Parity is 0.95; the decay-waste haircut lands
   0.9 (a defensible +12.5 % nudge). `/deck-tuning` remains the empirical
   court for fine-tuning.
2. **CONCEDE = flat +3 capstone, NOT scaled to `concedeFloorFor`.** The
   moment the term reads the enemy's difficulty floor (8/10/12) it becomes
   enemy-aware → that is 36b. 36a stays a literal. +3 matches the
   `forgePersistence` / `echoNextSpell` "decisive persistent state-change"
   tier (defensible range 2–4).
3. **Premise currency stays 0.8.** Repricing it double-counts and is
   enemy-dependent (see the asymmetry above).
4. **Enchant/disenchant deferred to 36c.** Structural blocker (prose field,
   not a payload); pricing them without a real pricer just moves the
   blindness. Keep the "=0" assertion honest.
5. **No proximity multiplier.** An alt-win-*proximity* term needs the
   enemy's live resolve/HP → enemy-dependent → 36b. Ruled out.
6. **Verify scopes to mechanics.** Pricing is mechanics-internal, not in the
   barrel, not consumed by mobile — the coupling rule does not trigger.
