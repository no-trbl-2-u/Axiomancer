# TURN-LEVEL DECISION TEXTURE — is one combat turn a puzzle worth leaning into?

Auditor: mechanics-expert (SNOB lens), tuning-audit fan-out, 2026-07-10.
Sources: `combat.engine.ts` / `combat.cli.ts` / `combat.encounter.sim.ts` traces; 4 sim
invocations this pass (3 auto transcripts incl. one full `--state-log` event capture,
1 preset playtest with `--cards`); shared dossier + baseline. Doctrine and the
owner-locked dice law are treated as fixed; every proposal builds on them.

---

## Verdict up front

A turn of Axiomancer combat, as the engine actually executes it, is not a puzzle. It is
a vending machine with one button that works. The census below finds **at most one
genuinely live decision per typical turn** (which color to draft, and even that is
usually dictated by the hand), the baseline's greedy-vs-blind gap of **0pp at every
stage** proves the hidden-information layer is decorative, and chaos-play's 100% mid-stage
win rate proves the card-choice layer is too. Worse, this audit found that the engine
does not even agree with itself about what a "turn" *is*: the sims and auto-CLI take
**unlimited dice-turns per enemy phase**, while the interactive loop grants exactly one —
so every number in the baseline was generated by a bot playing a materially more
generous game than the one a human is offered. Slay the Spire's turn is a knapsack
problem; Into the Breach's turn is a proof obligation; Wildfrost's turn is a timeline
negotiation. Axiomancer's turn is currently a ritual: roll, take the forced draft, spam
the one wet card, feed the Conviction meter, press the signature button when it glows.

---

## 1. Anatomy of one turn — the honest census of live options

Walking the engine (`combat.engine.ts`) phase by phase, with the three transcripts as
witnesses (erosion vs the-butcher early seed 3; standstill vs rawhead-rex mid seed 5;
oratory vs king-of-revenge early seed 7, full event log via `--state-log`).

### 1a. Roll → draft (engine.ts:411 `startTurn`, :480 `draftStanceDie`)

Three dice, faces heart/body/mind/wild at 1/6 each, X at 2/6 — expected tray: **2
colored dice and 1 dead X**. The draft decision is "keep one, the rest become tokens."
Nominal options: up to 3. Live options: **usually one**, because three forces converge
on the same pick:

- **The color law makes the hand choose the die, not the player.** A die powers only
  its color (engine.ts:1143-1148, hard fizzle). A preset hand of 6 is 2-3 colors; the
  die that unlocks the most/best PAID lines is almost always unique. `chooseDraft`
  (engine.ts:2854-2873) encodes exactly this — color-match first, advantage-seek a
  distant second — and this trivial heuristic *ties the informed policy* (greedy = blind,
  0pp at all four stages, baseline §1b).
- **The read is a lottery you learn about after buying the ticket.** The enemy's phase
  stance is hidden and is revealed *at draft time, after commitment*
  (engine.ts:571-575 "Reveal the phase's hidden stance on first contest"). Playing
  *against* the telegraph's stance is impossible on first contact by construction;
  paying 1◆ for `sig-read-opponent` to peek is measurably worthless (0pp gap).
- **Token accrual is flat.** Every unpicked colored die = +1◆ regardless of color
  (engine.ts:557-565); the only differentiation is wild (+2◆). There is no "this die is
  worth more as a token" tension.

The one flicker of texture here is R5's free omen (engine.ts:504-516): an *unpicked*
die that beats the current stance scouts the next phase — automatic, no choice
involved. Information arrives as a passive drip, never as a purchase decision.

In the oratory event log, turns 4-14 read, verbatim: `turn-dice-rolled → die-drafted
(wild) → conviction-gained → conviction-gained → [nothing] → turn-dice-rolled…` — six
consecutive "turns" containing zero card plays and zero decisions, farming ◆ for the
next `sig-conviction-strike`. That is the actual texture of a mid-fight turn.

### 1b. Card plays — FREE/PAID under the color law (engine.ts:1058, :1091)

Hand of 6, each card printing a FREE and a PAID line, plus the sideways discard for +1◆
(engine.ts:698, spec 27's Mage-Knight salvage). Nominal action space: ~18 lines.
Live action space:

- PAID lines legal this turn: only cards matching the drafted color (or wild draft) —
  typically 2-4 of 6. Off-color PAID lines are not "expensive," they are **vetoed**.
- Among legal PAID plays, the variety chain (engine.ts:2012-2022: a NEW status refreshes
  the die; a repeat spends it) fully determines optimal ordering: fresh statuses first,
  repeats last. There is no tension in this ordering — it is a sort, not a choice.
  The erosion playtest witness: **straw-mans-jab alone is 71% of all enemy HP loss**
  (dom > 70% = the tool's own "spam" flag), 866 plays across 150 fights.
- FREE lines are played, in the bot's own code comments, as a **"drain"** — the fizzle
  fallback (combat.cli.ts:316-319, sim:301-307). In the erosion mid matrix, top-line
  plays are 534 of 3,040 plays (~18%), concentrated on the cards whose PAID line had
  nothing to do (currys-conversion: 185 tops, and its 288 bottoms landed **zero**
  statuses). Nobody *chooses* a FREE line; they fall back onto it.

### 1c. Signatures, fate tap, end of turn

- Signature cast at threshold (sim:218-224, cli:275-281): a meter, not a decision.
  Consequence below (§4).
- Fate tap (engine.ts:618): "+1 DoT tick or +1◆" — a real micro-choice, correctly
  gated once/turn; the sim resolves it with a one-line rule and loses nothing.
- Bank vs burn at draft/endTurn (engine.ts:523-530, :596-605): bank is strictly right
  whenever Reserve has room and ◆ isn't starved — again a rule, not a dilemma.

**Census total: a typical turn presents 1 (occasionally 2) genuinely live options.** The
baseline's two witnesses — greedy=blind (0pp × 4 stages) and chaos ≥ greedy at mid
(100% vs 94%) — are exactly what this census predicts. Every turn has an obvious best
line, and most turns' best line is also reachable by a coin flip.

## 2. FINDING (structural, new): the game does not know what a turn is

The owner-locked dice law says **"3 dice rolled/round, player applies 1 rolled
die/round."** The engine implements "1 die per *turn*" and then lets callers take
**unlimited turns per round**:

- `startTurn` (engine.ts:411-416) gates only on `phase === 'phase-play'` and "no
  drafted die." Nothing couples `state.turn` to `state.round`. After `endTurn`, a
  caller may immediately `startTurn` again — fresh 3-die roll, fresh token income —
  without the enemy ever acting.
- The engine's own top-level entry point *institutionalizes* this:
  `resolveCombatPhase` (engine.ts:2897-2901) — "Bottom plays auto-manage turns" — and
  `ensureDraftForCard` (engine.ts:2876-2894) end the turn and re-roll whenever the die
  doesn't suit the next card in the batch.
- The sim harness (combat.encounter.sim.ts:248-252) and the auto CLI
  (combat.cli.ts:286-304) both loop startTurn/endTurn inside a single enemy phase, up
  to a 60-iteration guard.
- The **interactive** loop (combat.cli.ts:397-482) rolls **once per enemy phase**: one
  draft, one card loop, resolve threat.

Witness: the oratory state-log shows **14 `turn-dice-rolled` events across 3 enemy
rounds** (~4.7 player turns per enemy action). Each extra turn banks ~2◆ from unpicked
dice, so the bot's Conviction income runs at ~8-10◆/round against the intended
~2◆/round. This is why `sig-conviction-strike` (7◆) delivers **85-100% of all damage in
all three transcripts** (648/766 vs the-butcher; **2,224/2,224** vs rawhead-rex — the
standstill deck's own 15 cards contributed *zero* HP; 2,064/2,598 vs king-of-revenge).
The signature isn't overtuned so much as it is being fed by a rules violation.

Consequences:

1. **Every baseline number is suspect.** The 99/94/3/17 curve, the statusEngagement
   decay, the alt-win death — all measured under ~5× the legal dice/Conviction income.
   The human-facing game (one roll per phase) is dramatically poorer in resources and
   has never been measured.
2. **The owner-locked law is currently unenforceable doctrine** — prose, not code.
3. Turn-level decision texture cannot even be evaluated until the game commits to one
   answer. A Wildfrost turn is tense *because* the timeline advances after each of your
   limited actions; a turn that can be extended indefinitely while the enemy waits has
   no timeline at all.

This is the single highest-leverage fix in this report (P1).

## 3. FINDING: the color law doesn't constrain the read — it eats it

Two rules govern the same resource (the die's color): the **color law** (binary veto on
PAID plays) and the **read** (±1 intensity/duration, +1◆ on advantage —
engine.ts:188-189, 1254-1268). A veto always dominates a modifier. Drafting off-color
to win the read costs you *every* on-color PAID line in hand; the read's reward
(+1 intensity on this turn's fresh stacks) never pays for that. So the rational player
ignores the stance contest entirely — which is precisely what the 0pp greedy-vs-blind
gap measures. The "read your opponent" fantasy (the drafted die as a stance contest,
Sekiro's posture dialogue) is dead on arrival: the color law reduced it to a
color-matching mini-rule with a vestigial dice-RPS bolted on.

Note the design shape: this is *not* an argument to revert the color law. The law is a
good constraint — it makes dice non-fungible and makes FORGE/Reserve/wild meaningful.
The problem is that the read competes with it for the same choice. They must be
decoupled (P3): the read should reward something **orthogonal to color** so that
"off-color die that beats the stance" is a live temptation rather than a trap.

## 3b. FINDING: the telegraph is scenery, not a puzzle

Is the enemy telegraph read and played against? No — three independent proofs:

- **You cannot see it when it matters.** The phase stance reveals *after* the draft
  (engine.ts:571-575). The threat's damage/rungs are visible, but the one attribute the
  dice system keys on (stance) is hidden at decision time.
- **Nobody who answers it is rewarded.** The policies that engage with the telegraph
  are the worst in the game: control-lock 49% avg at mid, turtle 13%, while chaos —
  which ignores the telegraph by definition — scores 100% (baseline §1c). Rawhead-rex,
  the one enemy whose burst demands an answer, is beaten by random play (100%) and lost
  by the doctrine policy (dot-weaver 0%).
- **Fights end before the telegraph exists.** Erosion vs the-butcher: victory in
  **1 phase** — the enemy never acted. Early fights average 2.0-2.5 rounds; a threat
  sequence with no future is not a telegraph, it's a title card.

Spec 29 (reactive enemies) diagnosed this itself — "the 'static puzzle' risk… fights
resolve before any real adaptation is demanded" — and **both spec 29 and spec 30 (the
lethality readout) are still `Status: Draft — handoff`, unimplemented.** The
Sekiro/Into-the-Breach loop the specs promise (see the threat, answer the threat, feel
the answer) has no engine surface today. STAGGER/BACKFIRE/GUARD exist as verbs, but
nothing makes answering *this* phase's specific threat better than blindly stacking DoT.

## 3c. FINDING: the FREE line — diagnosis of the owner's signal

The owner: *"I really want Axiomancer to FUNDAMENTALLY do away with these 'do low basic
damage OR a status effect' forks."*

Diagnosis from the spec 32 §7 catalogue (all 50 spells): the strike is dead on PAID
lines, but the FREE line quietly resurrected it. Of 50 spell FREE lines:

- **9 are `tick`** (slippery-slope, straw-mans-jab, festering-argument,
  resonance-detonation, sweet-poison, self-flagellant, brief-candle, winnowing,
  the-reaping) — an immediate damage instance. TICK is chip damage in DoT clothes: it
  advances no counter, builds no board, and is worth 0 with no DoT up. This is the
  fork the owner smells.
- **~14 are `draw 1`** — generic, theme-blind filler that competes with the sideways
  discard (+1◆, engine.ts:698) for the same "dead card" job. Spec 27 already ships the
  Mage-Knight salvage valve; a FREE line that is merely "draw 1" is redundant
  infrastructure.
- **7 are `Guard 2/3`**, five of them on non-Bulwark cards (zenos-half-step,
  arrow-paradox, the-olive-branch, pact-of-akrasia, the-overtake) — generic again.
- **~20 conform to the right pattern already**: +1 Premise (exordium,
  opening-statement, mounting-case, the-closing-word), FORETELL 1 (glimpse,
  signs-and-portents, delphic-ambiguity, prophecy-fulfilled), SWAY 1
  (disarming-smile, heart-of-the-matter), +1 Soul (memento-mori), +1◆
  (bootstrap-loop, against-my-judgment), heal 2 / mark d1…

The conforming half proves the concept: Peroration's FREE lines are pure foundation
(+1 Premise feeds PERORATION/CONCEDE), and in the oratory transcript the bot happily
played them. The generic half is why FREE plays are "drain" clicks. Spec 32's own
budget law (§2: "FREE ≈ 25-35% of total points") prices the FREE line but never
*shapes* it — nothing in the spec requires the FREE line to build toward the PAID line.

**What the FREE line should be (P4): the theme's currency, always, by law.** FREE never
deals damage, never generically draws; it deposits one unit of the theme's foundation
so that every FREE play is a down-payment on a PAID payoff. (Table in P4.)

One more turn-texture witness for why foundation must *cash*: the oratory defeat ended
with **21 Premises banked and zero Perorations fired** (state-log, final snapshot —
`premises: 21`, defeat at round 3). The-closing-word (the only cash-out) is a 1-of rare
the bot never drew-and-powered. A currency with one cashier in 15 cards is a trap; the
FREE-currency law needs at least one payoff *per common pair* (P4 addresses).

## 3d. FINDING (confirmed): the color-match bonuses are dead weight on every card face

Confirmed fold-in candidates, exactly as the handoff flagged (HANDOFF-2026-07-09:145-147):

- `colorMatch` (engine.ts:1161) is `wild || color === card.stance` — but step 1b
  (engine.ts:1143) already fizzled every play that doesn't satisfy exactly that.
  **colorMatch is tautologically true for every resolving play except fate-X.**
- `COLOR_MATCH_DAMAGE_BONUS = 3` (engine.ts:90) now only fires on guard/barrier gains
  (engine.ts:2091, 2096) — every Guard 8 is really Guard 11, every BARRIER 10 is 13.
- `COLOR_MATCH_STATUS_DURATION_BONUS = 1` (engine.ts:200, applied :1356) — every
  printed duration on a status card is a lie by exactly +1 turn.

A near-unconditional bonus is not a bonus; it is a tax on the card face's honesty
(the P0-truth doctrine this codebase repeatedly invokes). Fold both into printed
numbers and delete the constants (P5). The only behavioral wrinkle: fate-X plays
currently *miss* both bonuses; folding in gives fate-X plays +1 duration/+3 guard for
free. Given fate lines already pay in recoil and rarity, accept it — or dock printed
fate riders by one unit where it matters.

## 4. Why the signature button is the whole game (and what that does to turns)

`sig-conviction-strike` (7◆, guaranteed boosted poison, combat.signature.ts:50-53)
delivered 85-100% of damage in all three transcripts. Three compounding causes, in
order of blame:

1. The unlimited-turns bug (§2) quintuples ◆ income for bots.
2. Dice-law rework legitimately ~doubled ◆ income (handoff:148-150) with no signature
   cost repricing.
3. Signatures are hand-independent and color-law-exempt — the one action that never
   fizzles — so every heuristic (and every rational human) routes power through them.

Turn-texture consequence: the *library* — the 70 cards, the ten themes, the thing the
player builds — is relegated to garnish. A turn where the best play is almost always
"bank tokens toward the button" is a turn with no reason to look at your hand. Fix
order matters: enforce P1 first, re-measure, *then* reprice (P6) — repricing against
the current poisoned baseline would be tuning a mirage.

## 5. Proposals

**P1 — ENFORCE THE ROUND-TURN LAW (the prerequisite for everything).** Engine-level:
`startTurn` no-ops (or hard-errors) if a dice-turn was already taken this round; add
`turnTakenRound` to state, reset in `processBetweenPhases`. Delete the re-roll loops in
`combat.encounter.sim.ts:248-252` / `combat.cli.ts:286-304`; `ensureDraftForCard` may
end the turn but not start a new one mid-phase. The floating-dice/Reserve/refresh
economy already exists to make one draft enough — this is what those systems are *for*
(spec 32 §5's "bigger turns"). Then re-run the entire baseline; every curve number,
statusEngagement figure, and alt-win rate must be re-derived. Effort: M.

**P2 — SHOW THE STANCE BEFORE THE DRAFT.** Move the current phase's stance reveal from
`draftStanceDie` (engine.ts:571-575) to `startTurn`. Full information about *now*, à la
Into the Breach; secrecy lives in phases n+1+ (Oracle's FORETELL, R5 omens, and
spec 29's reactive swaps stay valuable). The draft becomes an informed trilemma —
match my hand / beat the revealed stance / bank the wild — which is the first time the
3-dice draft can host a real decision. Costs nothing mechanically: the bots that
already peek (greedy) prove this changes ~nothing in win rate; what it changes is that
a *human* can finally reason about the pick. Effort: S.

**P3 — DECOUPLE THE READ FROM THE COLOR LAW.** The read's reward must be orthogonal to
color so beating the stance with an off-color die is a live line, not a trap. Concrete
version: **advantage ⇒ the drafted die acts WILD this turn** (the win *earns* universal
color); disadvantage ⇒ the enemy's telegraph gains +1 rung this phase. Now the draft
trilemma of P2 has teeth: the body die that beats a revealed mind stance unlocks your
whole hand, at the risk-free price of nothing — while a safe on-color pick is the
coward's steady income. (Keeps THE COLOR LAW intact: wild-via-read is an earned
exception exactly like the gold face, not a revert.) Kill `CONVICTION_READ_WIN_BONUS`
in the same pass; the read should pay in tempo, not tokens. Effort: M.

**P4 — THE FREE-CURRENCY LAW (the owner's signal, made structural).** Spec-32
amendment: *a spell's FREE line always deposits one unit of its theme's foundation
currency; it never deals damage (no `tick`), never generically draws.* Per-theme
currency map:

| theme | FREE deposit (replaces) | feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Affliction | MARK 1 (was tick ×4) | RUPTURE fuel, tick amps |
| Peroration | +1 Premise (already conforms) | PERORATION / CONCEDE |
| Forge | +1 PIP to a held die (was draw/guard) | payoff verbs, the-overtake |
| Akrasia | self-mark 1 "credit" (was tick ×2) | FALLEN gates |
| Control | FRAY 1 — a rung dent that regrows next phase unless STAGGER locks it (was draw/guard) | STAGGER/BACKFIRE |
| Oracle | FORETELL 1 (already conforms) | OMEN hits |
| Harvest | +1 Soul (was tick ×3) | REAP |
| Charm | SWAY 1 (already conforms) | CAPITULATE |
| Bulwark | Guard 2 (already conforms — guard IS the currency) | RIPOSTE full-block |
| Echo | mill 1 card to discard (was draw) | REPRISE targets |

Also: give each theme a second cash-out below rare (the 21-stranded-Premises lesson) —
e.g. peroratio-interrupta already is one; audit the other nine themes for a common/
uncommon payoff. TICK survives as a PAID rider and on the fate tap, where it belongs.
This makes every FREE click a visible step toward the theme's fantasy — the fork stops
being "weak chip vs real play" and becomes "build now vs spend now," which is the StS
energy question, the only question worth asking every turn. Effort: L.

**P5 — FOLD THE MATCH BONUSES.** Add +1 to printed durations on status spells, +3 to
printed guard/barrier amounts; delete `COLOR_MATCH_DAMAGE_BONUS`,
`COLOR_MATCH_STATUS_DURATION_BONUS`, and the `colorMatch` plumbing (engine.ts:90, 200,
1161, 1356, 2091, 2096). Card faces become P0-true. Fate-X plays inherit the folded
numbers; accept the +1/+3 as fate's small dignity. Effort: S.

**P6 — REPRICE THE SIGNATURE ECONOMY *AFTER* P1.** With legal income (~2-3◆/round),
re-tune: `sig-conviction-strike` likely lands at 8-9◆ or gains a once-per-fight cap;
`convictionThreshold` policies re-derived. Do not touch numbers before P1 lands — the
current 90-95% damage share is mostly the exploit's shadow. Effort: M (mostly re-baselining).

**P7 — SHIP THE MINIMUM SLICE OF SPECS 29/30.** One reactive verb (cleanse OR harden,
spec 29 §4) plus the spec 30 lethality readout ("lethal in N phases"). Together they
create the turn question this audit found missing everywhere: *race it or answer it?*
— visible, per-turn, and finally a reason the telegraph exists. Effort: L.

**P8 — GIVE AUDITORS EYES.** `--auto` must emit the per-phase narration / `autoPhase`
events to stdout (combat.cli.ts:397-481 exists only interactively; baseline flagged the
same gap). The `--state-log` full-event workaround used here (1.8 MB of JSON per fight)
is not a transcript tool. Also fix attribution accounting ("71 phases" in a 5-phase
fight; 2,598 DoT attributed vs a 150 HP enemy). Effort: S.

## 6. Priority argument

P1 is not one proposal among eight; it is the gate. Until the round-turn law is code,
the game being tuned is not the game being played, and no turn-texture change can be
evaluated. P2+P3 are the cheapest route to a draft worth 10 seconds of thought
(informed trilemma with an earned-wild carrot). P4 is the owner's signal made law and
converts ~26 dead FREE lines into theme progress. P5 is hygiene. P6/P7 restore the
library and the telegraph as the sources of damage and of fear, respectively. The
target state: a turn where the player looks at a revealed threat, three dice, a hand of
currency-vs-payoff forks, and a floating-die bank — and cannot decide in under ten
seconds. Today they can decide in zero, because there is nothing to decide.
