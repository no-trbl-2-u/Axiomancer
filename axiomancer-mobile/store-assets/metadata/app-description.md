# Axiomancer Mobile App Description

## Short Description (80 chars max)
A dark gothic TTRPG companion app with turn-based combat and character building.

## Long Description

**Axiomancer Mobile** brings the immersive world of tabletop role-playing to your device with a dark gothic aesthetic and deep character customization.

**Key Features:**
- **Turn-based Combat**: Strategic battles with stance-based mechanics and tactical decision making
- **Character Progression**: Build your character with skills, equipment, and meaningful stat development  
- **Rich Exploration**: Navigate detailed environments and discover hidden secrets
- **Inventory Management**: Collect, craft, and equip weapons, armor, and consumables
- **Event-driven Storytelling**: Experience branching narratives and meaningful choices
- **Offline Play**: Complete TTRPG experience with no internet required
- **Dark Gothic Theme**: Atmospheric design with medieval fantasy aesthetics

**Perfect for fans of:**
- Tabletop RPGs and turn-based strategy games
- Character building and progression systems
- Dark fantasy and medieval settings
- Single-player story-driven experiences

**Privacy Focused:**
- No account creation required
- All progress saved locally on your device
- No data collection or online connectivity needed

Dive into a world of tactical combat, character growth, and gothic storytelling designed for both newcomers and experienced TTRPG enthusiasts.
