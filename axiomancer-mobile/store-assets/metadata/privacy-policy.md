# Axiomancer Mobile Privacy Policy

## Data Collection
Axiomancer Mobile does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information.

## Local Storage Only
- All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device
- No user accounts or registration required
- No online connectivity or cloud sync features
- No analytics, tracking, or usage data collection

## Third-Party Services
This app does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy
As we do not collect any personal information, this app is safe for users of all ages under applicable privacy laws including COPPA.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
Any updates to this privacy policy will be included in app updates distributed through official app stores.

## Contact
For privacy-related questions, contact the development team through the official app store listing.
