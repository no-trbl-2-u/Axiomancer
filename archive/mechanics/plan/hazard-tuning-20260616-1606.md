## 1. Headline deviation from targets

**Gold-vow and CHOOSE utility cards are an anti-synergy trap: acquiring them
makes a deck measurably *worse*, the opposite of what the utility-deck doctrine
demands.** This is the central utility-deck balance failure this tick found.

| Deck (vs. starter baseline) | Safe Δ avg wins | Risk Δ avg wins |
|---|---|---|
| `+ auras` (free row carries numbers)   | **+0.62** | **+0.88** |
| `+ codex utilities` (transmute/ward/…) | +0.23 | +0.22 |
| `+ bursts`                             | +0.02 | 0.00 |
| **`+ gold-vow / CHOOSE`**              | **−1.01** | **−0.65** |

The deficit is consistent across **all 6 hazards and both routes** (−0.85…−0.95
safe, −0.58…−0.62 risk; clear-rate drop 14–25 pp). Auras — the utility class
with a non-zero *free* row — are the strongest and most fun acquisition; the
all-gold-gated classes punish the player.

**Doctrine link:** CLAUDE.md / VISION.md require utility-deck play to be *more*
engaging than flat number trading. A reward-pool card class that reliably lowers
win rate when played is a doctrine violation even though starter win/loss rates
look healthy.

### Utility-card number audit (the smoking gun)

```
r_aggr   red    aura     f/e=3/0  fp/ep=6/0     <- aura: non-zero free row
r_swift  blue   aura     f/e=0/3  fp/ep=0/6
r_zeal   purple aura     f/e=2/2  fp/ep=2/2
r_martyr gold   aura     f/e=0/0  fp/ep=6/6     <- gold aura: 0/0 free, gold-gated
r_serk   red    burst    f/e=0/0  fp/ep=0/0     <- burst: 0/0 free
r_vow    gold   goldvow  f/e=0/0  fp/ep=0/0     <- vow: 0/0 free, gold-gated
r_twin   gold   choose   f/e=0/0  fp/ep=8/8     <- choose: 0/0 free, gold-gated
x_pendulum  gold choose  f/e=0/0  fp/ep=8/8
x_eithergate gold choose f/e=0/0  fp/ep=8/8
```

**Root cause:** the gold-vow (`r_vow`, `x_goldoath`, `x_brightpromise`) and
CHOOSE (`r_twin`, `x_pendulum`, `x_eithergate`) cards have a **0/0 free row AND
require a *gold* die** to do anything. Gold is 2 of 6 die faces; with 4 dice cast
once per hazard, the expected gold supply is ~1.3 dice/round, and a hand often
holds 2–3 gold-gated cards competing for it. They therefore brick frequently —
sitting in the play area contributing nothing — which drags the whole deck below
baseline. Auras avoid this because their free row already pays out.

## 4. Change decision — propose-only (nothing applied)

The vow/choose deficit is **structural, not a magnitude problem.** The only
in-registry numeric levers for these cards are `cards.expansion.vow`
(`{force:7, escape:7}`) and `cards.expansion.choose` (`8`) — the values they pay
*when a gold die lands on them*. Raising those does **not** reduce the brick rate;
it only makes the lucky hits bigger, i.e. it adds variance to a problem that is
already a variance trap. More feast-or-famine is *worse* for engagement, not
better. Applying such a bump would be tuning a number to paper over an engine/
content-shape gap — explicitly disallowed (skill §3, "Known engine gaps are not
tuning levers"). So **no numeric change is applied this tick.** This is a
deliberate "propose, don't apply" outcome, not an omission.

### Propose-only (needs T approval — structural / content-shape)

1. **Give the gold-vow and CHOOSE cards a small non-zero free row** (e.g.
   `f/e = 2/2`, mirroring the purple-aura `r_zeal` shape), so an un-powered copy
   is a modest dual card instead of dead weight. This is the single highest-impact
   fix: it converts the brick from "−value" to "+small value", which the evidence
   shows is exactly what separates auras (winners) from vows (losers). Estimated
   to move `+vow/choose` from −1.0 wins toward parity-or-better, in line with the
   aura/codex classes. *Content-shape change to card defs in `hazard.content.ts`
   — propose-only.*
2. **Alternatively / additionally, relax the gold-gate**: let CHOOSE accept any
   colour die (it already pays one chosen meter), and let GILDED VOW ride the
   first die of *any* colour rather than gold-only. This attacks the scarcity
   directly. *Engine/`dieCanPowerCard` behaviour — propose-only.*
3. **Reward-offer curation guard (engagement safety):** `generateRewardOffer`
   (`hazard.engagement.ts`) classifies a deck and offers a `gold-utility` aligned
   card as the "obvious benefit" slot. Because vow/choose are `gold-utility`, a
   gold-leaning deck is actively *tempted toward the trap*. Until (1)/(2) land,
   consider excluding the 0/0-free gold-gated cards from the Slot-A "obvious
   benefit" pool so the curation never recommends a deck-weakening pick.

### Considered but not applied

- `cards.expansion.vow {7,7} → {9,9}` and `choose 8 → 10`: increases swing on
  connect but not reliability; evidence says it can't close a ~1.0-win gap without
  becoming broken on the lucky rounds. Rejected as anti-doctrine (adds variance).
- Global `dice.faces` gold-count increase (e.g. add a 3rd gold face): would help
  every gold-gated card, but it is a high-blast-radius round-shape knob that lifts
  the band-guarded starter balance (safe perfect already sits as low as 28 % for
  ashfall/fever — near the divergence-doc 0.35 floor). Too risky to apply blind in
  the same tick; left as a future A/B if (1)/(2) are declined.

## 5. Engine / harness gaps recorded

1. **Greedy bot never plays free-row-less utilities.** `hazard.sim.ts`
   (`playGreedyRound`) and `hazard.cli.ts --auto` only explicitly fire `draw` /
   `recast` / `convert`; bursts, auras, gold-vows, and CHOOSE are scored by
   `freeValueToward`, which returns 0 for a 0/0 free row, so they are never staged
   and their effects never fire. **Consequence:** the shipped balance sim and the
   `--auto` CLI are *blind to the entire acquired-utility-deck axis* — they cannot,
   by construction, measure the focus of this tick. The evidence here required a
   custom utility-aware policy. This is the reason no CLI-log citation appears
   below; the CLI cannot reach the metric.

2. **`npm run hazard` CLI does not run (pre-existing, unrelated).** `ts-node`
   fails to compile `src/CLI/game.cli.ts:185` — `describeResolvedEvent` "lacks an
   ending return statement" under ts-node's isolated-modules view of the
   `ResolvedEvent` union. **`npx tsc --noEmit` passes clean**, so `npm run verify`
   is unaffected; the break is confined to the ts-node CLI entrypoint. Not a hazard
   file and not touched here (CLI/engine surface = T-approval). Flagged so the next
   worker is not surprised that `npm run hazard` errors.

3. **No CLI flag injects an acquired/utility bag.** `hazard.cli.ts` builds the
   draw bag from `hazardStarterBag()` only; there is no way to add reward/codex
   cards to a `--auto` run. Even with gap (2) fixed, the CLI still could not
   exercise utility-deck strategies. See harness-gap entry in
   `plan/PHASE_CANDIDATES.md`.
