## Why no numeric change was applied

- **Axis A (mana-crisis 10% vs >50%)** — policy/structural, not a numeric value.
  With `E[hex]=0.67` and 2 gold faces, dice are not scarce enough at baseline for
  a 50% crisis to be natural. This is a die-economy/re-cast doctrine question, not
  a tuning-surface lever. **Propose-only.**
- **Axis B (safe final round not completable)** — CDR-0006 says "always
  completable"; the shipped test bands deliberately make a perfect run uncommon
  (R3 threshold 25–26 sits above the no-mana free-value ceiling ~22–23). Lowering
  R3 thresholds would lift `perfectRate` over the test ceiling (0.52) and reverse
  committed intent. **Conflicts — needs T to choose which doctrine wins.**
- **Axis C (direct-progress 64% > 50%)** — the real flat-round lever and the most
  vision-relevant (more utility/convert/recast in hand = more combo play). But
  reaching ≤50% needs ~4 starter-copy reshuffles, which moves the balance-sim bag
  and very likely breaks `hazard.balance.sim.test.ts`. **Propose-only** until the
  bands are deliberately re-cut with T sign-off.
- **Axis D (CONVERT draw 36.7% < 40%)** — cleanest single knob
  (`windread weight 2→3` → ~48%) and within the tuning surface, but **moot**: the
  hex target for CONVERT essentially never appears (1/6 hex), so the card rarely
  has anything to convert. Not worth chasing the 40% number.
